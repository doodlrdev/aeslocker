Privacy Policy for AESLocker Pro

Last updated: September 2026

AESLocker Pro ("the App") is developed and maintained by Doodlr ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

Summary

AESLocker Pro does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The App does not request internet access and performs all file encryption and decryption operations entirely on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect or have access to:

  • Your files, passwords, or encryption keys
  • Your name, email, or contact information
  • Device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics
  • Any information via third-party SDKs, ads, or analytics services (the App contains none)

Permissions

The App requests only the storage/file access permission required to encrypt and decrypt files you choose within the App. It does not request the Internet permission and cannot send data anywhere, even if it wanted to.

Third-Party Services

AESLocker Pro does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or other SDKs. The App also does not use Google Play's In-App Update or In-App Review services — those are exclusive to the free version of the app.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to This Policy

If our data practices ever change (for example, if a future update adds an optional online feature), this policy will be updated accordingly, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
