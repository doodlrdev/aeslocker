Privacy Policy for AESLocker Pro

Last updated: September 2026

AESLocker Pro ("the App") is developed and maintained by Doodlr ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user information.

Summary

AESLocker Pro does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The App does not request internet access and performs all file encryption and decryption operations entirely offline on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, view, or have access to:

  • Your files, passwords, or encryption keys
  • Your name, email address, or contact details
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, location data, or usage metrics
  • Any information via third-party SDKs or analytics (the App contains none)

App Permissions

The App requests only local file/storage access permissions required to read and write the files you choose to encrypt or decrypt. It does not request the INTERNET permission and cannot communicate over a network.

Third-Party SDKs and Services

AESLocker Pro does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, tracking SDKs, or external cloud services.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect or transmit any data from any user.

Changes to This Policy

If our data practices ever change, this policy will be updated accordingly, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the revision date.
